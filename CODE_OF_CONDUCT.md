# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in GeoChannel a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Help maintain a focused and welcoming technical community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith engagement
- Publishing private information without permission
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/zamo24). Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct
is harmful to the project or its community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
